Disability Product Changes

How a new disability product or a new variation goes live, and what happens to claims that are already running. Most changes are data in the product configuration. Code changes only when a variation brings new events or a new shape of claim.

The products named here are labelled examples of common individual disability variations, not your actual book.

Three kinds of change, three rules for running claims

Every change is one of these. Which kind it is decides whether a claim already in progress sees it.

KindExamplesHow a claim gets its versionClaims already running
Contract termsElimination period, benefit period, definition of disability, riders, residual formulaFrom the policy form the policy was issued on, pinned on the benefit line at approvalNever change. The policy is a contract.
RegulationState claim-handling rules: letter timing, decision limits, interest on late paymentsBy effective dateChange on the effective date, for every open claim in that state
Internal handlingLetter wording, requirement sets, review cadence, automation rulesBy releaseChange from the release, unless compliance says a change would disadvantage the claimant

The change ladder

Four levels. The higher the level, the more parts change, and the more people sign off.

Checklist for any new variation

  1. 1 · Place it on the ladderThe level decides who signs off and whether engineering is involved.
  2. 2 · Define it in product configurationRecord which policy forms it applies to and its effective date.
  3. 3 · List the deadlines each state needsThe nightly check then knows what to expect from day one.
  4. 4 · Letters and requirement setsCompliance approves the wording and any state filings.
  5. 5 · Code, for level 3 and upNew deadline kinds, handlers and workflows, switched on only by the product configuration.
  6. 6 · SimulateRun sample and past claims through it and compare the results with the actuary's expectations.
  7. 7 · Go live on the effective dateNew claims pin the new version. Claims already running keep theirs.
  8. 8 · Watch the first claimsReview the first 90 days of claims with product and actuarial.
