One individual disability income claim, from filing to recovery. The picture shows which part of the backend owns each piece. The components are the ones on the Claims Backend Architecture page.

What goes where, over the life of the claim

Zoom in: one month of benefits

The split in four lines

  • Temporal owns the clocksAnything with a deadline or a wait on this one claim: elimination period, status letters, decision limit, monthly proof, anniversaries, recurrence window.
  • Postgres owns the factsClaim, documents, tasks, the locked decision, each month's calculation, payment instructions, audit events.
  • Batch owns files and totalsPayment files, bank returns, reserve extracts, re-screening, the nightly clock audit. Work over all claims at once.
  • They meet through a row or a signalA workflow hands work to batch by writing a row. Batch hands news to a workflow by sending a signal.

Based on docs/backend-architecture.md and the platform scope: individual disability income, including residual benefits. Durations are examples.
