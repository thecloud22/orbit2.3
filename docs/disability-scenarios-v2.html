Disability Claim Scenarios

Nine scenarios, each drawn as one swimlane. Every deadline is a row in the deadlines table. Temporal only runs short workflows: each one is started by a deadline coming due or by something that happened, finishes within minutes to days, and writes its result to Postgres. Red numbers mark where a scenario can break; the failure handling page explains each one.

Products, amounts and durations are labelled examples: a $5,000 monthly benefit, a 90-day elimination period, monthly proof, and a 6-month recurrence window.
