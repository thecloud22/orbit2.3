Claims Failure Handling

How the v2 design fails and how each failure is caught. The worst failures in claims are silent: a missed payment, an overpayment, a letter that never went out. So every failure has to reach a person within a known time, and every fix has to be safe to repeat.

Five layers, each slower and more expensive than the last

A failure that gets past one layer is caught by the next. The goal is to stop almost everything in the first three layers, within the hour.

Every failure, and what catches it

Numbers match the red markers on the scenario pages. Filled numbers are silent or costly: a missed payment, an overpayment, a regulatory breach. Outlined numbers are noisy and caught quickly.

The payment item: who can change it

Most money failures come down to this one record, so it gets its own state machine.

Checked every night

These checks don't prevent a failure. They make sure it shows up within a day instead of in a complaint to the Department of Insurance.

  • Every claim in a state has the deadlines that state requires.catches 115
  • No deadline is more than an hour overdue, and nothing stays dispatched for hours without finishing.catches 26
  • No outbox row is more than an hour old.catches 5
  • No document sits unmatched to a period for more than a day, and no proof waits past its review limit.catches 812
  • Every hold has a review deadline, and every suspension has a letter.catches 1216
  • No item is paid for a period after the claim's end date or date of death.catches 11
  • A sample of calculations, re-run, matches what was paid.catches 910
  • Paid items match bank confirmations to the cent.catches 1314
  • No insured has two open claims for the same disability dates.catches 21
