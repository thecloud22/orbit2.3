Intake to Decision

Everything between the first notice of a disability claim and the decision: the checks that run at intake, how evidence is requested and chased, which clocks the state rules start, who works on the claim, and how it hands over to monthly benefits.

Day counts are labelled examples. Acknowledgement, decision and status-letter limits come from each state's claim-handling rules, and the elimination period comes from the policy.

The whole path

Six stages. The claim's status moves along the Claims API lane; every clock sits in the Deadlines lane; Temporal only runs the short steps in between.

The first minutes: intake checks

One orchestration, started from the outbox when first notice is saved. It either sets the claim up or stops and hands the claim to a person.

Evidence: one requirement's life

Each piece of evidence is a requirement row: the claim forms, the authorization, a physician's statement, tax returns. Proof of loss is complete when every requirement is accepted or waived.

The clocks, from first notice to decision

One example claim. State rules start some clocks at first notice and others only when proof of loss is complete.

Every task lives in Postgres and shows in the work queues. When a person finishes a task, that's an event: it goes through the outbox and starts the next short workflow. No workflow sits waiting for a person.
